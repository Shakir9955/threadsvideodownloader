Troubleshooting Common Problems

The link is not recognized

Confirm that you copied the complete URL of a public Threads post rather than a profile URL. Remove any spaces or extra characters.

No video is detected

Make sure the post contains a video and remains publicly accessible. Private, removed, restricted, or region-limited media may not be available.

The download does not start

Check your browser's download and pop-up permissions, disable any extension that is blocking the page, and retry in an updated browser.

The downloaded file does not play

Confirm that the download completed successfully and open the file in a current media player. If the file is incomplete, download your authorized copy again.
